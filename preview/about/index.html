Yanming

Whole-body robotic dexterity / Embodied RSI

I am currently a research intern at HKU MMLab, mentored by Prof. Ping Luo. Previously, I interned at Shanghai AI Lab and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, mentored by Prof. Yao Mu.

Research vision

Towards human-level dexterity

I am interested in how a robot learns to solve a task that its current skills cannot handle. My focus is dexterous manipulation: using physical feedback to discover a first successful strategy, then turning that strategy into a skill that transfers to new objects and situations.

I study embodied recursive self-improvement through self-harness: an agent revises the code that coordinates its tools, memory, and skills, then tests those changes through interaction. I want to connect this process with learning from real and synthetic human data. Successful trajectories could train better action models; those models could, in turn, support exploration of harder tasks. My long-term goal is to understand what makes this cycle reliable and scalable toward human-level dexterity.
