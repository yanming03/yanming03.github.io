Research vision

Towards human-level
dexterity.

I am especially interested in "0-to-one" successful trajectory on difficult manipulation tasks. When its current policy fails, can a robot use interaction feedback to revise its control tools and compose existing skills into a solution?

My goal is to use real and synthetic human data to expand the tasks and environments robots can explore. Through self-harness, an agent would refine its tools, code, memory, and skills. Successful trajectories could then be distilled into action models, which become tools for further exploration. I want to understand how this cycle can scale toward human-level dexterity.
